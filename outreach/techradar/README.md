## What makes the pitch credible

Lead with working evidence, not category claims:

- deterministic public policy engine;
- `ALLOW / DENY / REQUIRE_APPROVAL` terminal demo;
- request-bound approvals and replay protection;
- concurrent-approval test against a daily cap;
- clear separation from x402, Solana, AP2 and other payment rails;
- explicit limitations rather than a claim of production readiness.
